# UNLOAD Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

UNLOAD ("we", "us") is an anonymous app where men post one line a night and other men answer. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we don't want to know who you are, and we built the app so we mostly can't.

Questions: **bout_sizable23@icloud.com**. UNLOAD is operated from Ontario, Canada.

## What we never collect

- Your name, email address, phone number or password. Sign in with Apple is set up so Apple does not send us your name or email.
- Your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
- Advertising identifiers. We don't run ads and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. There are none in version 1.0.

## What we do collect

| Data | Example | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple user identifier | A random string from Sign in with Apple, unique to UNLOAD | To keep you signed in and tie your Drops to your account |
| Your Drops and replies | "Money's tight and I haven't told her." | To show them to other users, which is the whole app |
| Respects, reports and blocks you make | You blocked a user; you reported a reply | To run the app and keep it safe |
| Onboarding choices | Burdens (Money, Work…), who you're becoming (Calm…) | To pick your nightly prompt and show it on your share card |
| Streak, challenge day and activity times | Day 6, last active yesterday | To count your streak and deliver Drops to men who were on in the last 48 hours |
| Reminder time and time zone | 9:00 PM, America/Chicago | To count your day in your own time zone |

Your Drops are shown to other users **without** any name or identifier. Other users can't see your Apple ID, your other Drops, or your streak.

## What stays on your phone

Only your sign-in session, stored in the iOS Keychain. The nightly reminder is a local notification scheduled by your phone; we don't send push notifications in version 1.0. When you share a Drop card, the image is made in memory and handed to the iOS share sheet; we don't save it.

## Moderation and safety

Every Drop and reply is checked automatically before anyone else sees it. We send the text (and nothing else, no identifier) to OpenAI's moderation service, and we also block phone numbers, social handles and links. If the check can't run, the text is not posted.

If a Drop looks like it involves self-harm or suicide, it is **not shown to anyone**. We show you a screen with one tap to call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. We keep that Drop marked as not delivered, visible only to you, so it never goes out; it is deleted with your account like everything else. A reply flagged this way is not saved at all. We do not contact anyone about it.

When someone reports content, we review it within 24 hours. Content reported by 3 different users is hidden automatically until reviewed. We may remove content or accounts that break the rules.

## Who processes data for us

| Provider | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Hosts our database, sign-in and server functions | All the data listed above |
| OpenAI | Checks text for harmful content | The text of each Drop and reply only |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple; App Store | Whatever Apple's own policy covers |
| Expo (EAS Update) | Delivers app updates | Standard request data like IP address and app version |

Our database is hosted in the United States. If you use UNLOAD from Canada, your data is stored and processed in the US.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising. OpenAI states that data sent to its API is not used to train its models. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When you tap **Settings → Delete account**, we delete your account, your Drops, your replies, your respects, and the reports and blocks you made, right away. Replies you wrote on other men's Drops are deleted too. Copies in encrypted database backups expire within 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete everything:** Settings → Delete account.
- **Stop reminders:** turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
- **Ask us anything** or request a copy of your data: email bout_sizable23@icloud.com. Because we don't know your name or email, we'll ask for details only the account holder would know, such as the exact text and date of a recent Drop.

If you're in California, Canada or another place with privacy laws, you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your data, and we don't sell or "share" it as those laws define it. We'll honor these requests for everyone.

## 18+ only

UNLOAD is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor is using UNLOAD, email us and we'll delete the account.

## Not therapy

UNLOAD is peer support between users. It is not therapy, counseling or a medical service. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (US and Canada) or call 911.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
