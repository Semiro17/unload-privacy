# UNLOAD Support


## Need help?

Email **bout_sizable23@icloud.com**. A real person reads every message, usually within 24 hours.

**If you're in crisis, don't wait for us.** Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US and Canada), or call **911**.

## What is UNLOAD?

Once a night you drop one anonymous line about what you're carrying. Other men answer with Respect or one line back. No name, no email, no profile. 18+ only.

UNLOAD is peer support, not therapy.

## Common questions

**Is it really anonymous?**
Yes. You sign in with Apple, and Apple doesn't send us your name or email. Other users see your words and nothing else.

**Who sees my Drop?**
A few men who were on recently, then anyone swiping the stack. Never someone picked because they know you.

**Why didn't my Drop send?**
Every Drop is checked first. Phone numbers, @handles and links are blocked, and so is hateful or harmful content. If your Drop sounded like you might hurt yourself, it wasn't sent, and we showed you how to reach 988 instead. Your streak still counts.

**Someone wrote something bad. What do I do?**
Tap the ⋯ on the card or reply and choose **Report** or **Block this man**. We review reports within 24 hours. Blocked users never see your Drops and you never see theirs.

**How do I change or stop the nightly reminder?**
Settings (the gear, top right) → Nightly nudge: pick 7 to 11 pm or Off. Or iOS Settings → Notifications → UNLOAD.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. Everything is wiped from our servers right away. This can't be undone.

**I lost my streak.**
A day counts when you Drop, in your own time zone. Email us if something looks wrong.

## Report a safety issue

If someone is in danger or a minor is using UNLOAD, email bout_sizable23@icloud.com with "SAFETY" in the subject. We read these first.
