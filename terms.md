_Last updated: November 25, 2026_


By using UNLOAD you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use the app.

## Who can use UNLOAD

You must be **18 or older**. One account per person.

## The rules

UNLOAD has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post:

- Threats, harassment, bullying or hate toward anyone, including for race, religion, sexuality, gender or disability.
- Sexual content, or anything involving minors.
- Encouragement of self-harm, suicide or violence.
- Phone numbers, social handles, links, real names or anything that identifies you or someone else.
- Spam, ads, scams or selling.
- Anything illegal.

Be a brother. Answer the way you'd want to be answered.

## What we do

Every Drop and reply is checked before anyone sees it. You can report or block on every card and reply. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content and ban accounts that break these rules, without warning. Content reported by 3 people is hidden until we review it.

## Not therapy, not emergency help

UNLOAD is peer support between strangers. It is not therapy, counseling or medical advice, and other users are not professionals. If you're in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text **988** (US and Canada) or call **911**.

## Your content

You own what you write. By posting, you let us show it to other UNLOAD users and process it to run and moderate the app. You can share your own Drop card wherever you like. Don't share other people's Drops.

## Ending

Delete your account any time in Settings. We can suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.
